    How to use it in a shop
    1. Hold a sheet of plain white paper in the same light as the item. Put the ring on it and tap Set white. Do this again in each new shop or light.
    2. Aim the ring at the item. Green means it matches your target code.
    3. The green marker shows the closest spot in view, so you can scan a shelf for the right one.
    4. Hold freezes the picture. Tap anywhere on it to read that spot.

    A phone camera is not a measuring instrument. Use this to pick the best candidate, then judge the final choice by eye in daylight.

    How to check a web page (computers)
    1. Open Color Match in its own window (in Chrome or Edge, install it from the address bar), and put it beside the browser you are using.
    2. Tap Share a page. Choose the Chrome Tab (or window) with the page you want to check, then Share. Use the browser's own button to switch to another tab later.
    3. Browse as usual. The picture here follows that tab. The green marker points at the closest spot on the page to your target.
    4. Click or drag on the picture to put the ring on any spot and read its exact color. Hold freezes the page.
    5. Open the Found tab: anything close to your target that stays on screen for a moment while you browse is collected there, best match first. The same item is not added twice when you scroll back to it. Keep the Color Match window visible (not minimized or behind another window), or it cannot see the page. The list stays on this computer and is gone when you close the window.

    Phones cannot share a screen. Photos on a page are shaded, so check the closest spot rather than expecting one flat color.
